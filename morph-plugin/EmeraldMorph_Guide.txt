==========================================================
  EMERALDMORPH 1.1.0 - EVERYTHING IT DOES
==========================================================

A standalone morph plugin for Paper 1.21.11. No other plugins
needed. Morphs are real entities, so they render correctly for
both Java players AND Bedrock players joining through Geyser.

HOW MORPHING WORKS
  When you morph, you turn invisible and a real mob with your
  name appears and copies your every movement. Other players
  see the mob, not you. The mob can't be hurt, traded with,
  pushed, or targeted by other mobs. You keep your own health,
  hitbox and abilities (it's a visual disguise - a creeper
  morph doesn't explode).

----------------------------------------------------------
  PLAYER COMMANDS
----------------------------------------------------------

/morph <mob>
    Become any mob. Tab-complete lists every option
    (all real mobs; the ender dragon is always blocked).
    Example: /morph creeper

/morph villager [biome] [job]
    Become a styled villager. Both extras are optional and
    work in either order.
      Biomes: desert, jungle, plains, savanna, snow, swamp, taiga
      Jobs:   armorer, butcher, cartographer, cleric, farmer,
              fisherman, fletcher, leatherworker, librarian,
              mason, nitwit, shepherd, toolsmith, weaponsmith
    Examples:
      /morph villager plains librarian
      /morph villager snow
      /morph villager farmer

/unmorph   (or /morph off)
    Change back to yourself.

/morphview
    Toggle whether YOU see your own morph mob. Hide it and it
    stops getting in the way of your clicks and aim - everyone
    else still sees it. Your choice is remembered.

----------------------------------------------------------
  OPERATOR COMMANDS  (OPs / emeraldmorph.admin)
----------------------------------------------------------

WHO CAN MORPH (fresh install = OPs only):
/allowmorph
    Everyone on the server may /morph (announced in chat).
/allowmorph <player>
    Give just that player access.
/unallowmorph
    Back to OP-only. Clears every grant AND changes every
    morphed non-OP back to a regular player.
/unallowmorph <player>
    Revoke one player (changes them back if morphed). Warns
    you if the everyone-switch is still on.

FORCED MORPHS (work even on players without access):
/morphall <mob> [biome] [job]
    Morph EVERY online player. e.g. /morphall chicken
/morphall off
    Change everyone back.
/morphplayer <player> <mob> [biome] [job]
    Morph one specific player. e.g. /morphplayer Steve villager
    swamp nitwit
/morphplayer <player> off
    Change that player back.

----------------------------------------------------------
  AUTOMATIC BEHAVIOUR
----------------------------------------------------------

- Morphs end on: logout, death, changing worlds, going into
  spectator mode, or the server stopping. Just morph again.
- Access grants and everyone-mode survive restarts
  (plugins/EmeraldMorph/data.yml).
- Nobody can open a trade window on a morphed "villager" or
  hurt/push the morph mob.
- Your floating name tag is hidden while morphed; the mob
  carries your name instead.

----------------------------------------------------------
  CONFIG  (plugins/EmeraldMorph/config.yml)
----------------------------------------------------------

blocked-mobs: []
    Extra mobs nobody may morph into, e.g.
      blocked-mobs:
        - WITHER
        - WARDEN
    The ender dragon is always blocked in code (it ignores
    the no-AI flag and would fly around wrecking things).

----------------------------------------------------------
  KNOWN QUIRKS
----------------------------------------------------------

- Worn armor and held items still render on your invisible
  body (a vanilla invisibility quirk). Stow armor for the
  clean look.
- The morph is cosmetic: no mob abilities, no mob hitbox.
- Don't run this alongside another plugin that registers
  /morph (e.g. EmeraldPerks' built-in morph) - the commands
  will clash. One morph system per server.

INSTALL: drop EmeraldMorph-1.1.0.jar into plugins/, restart.
